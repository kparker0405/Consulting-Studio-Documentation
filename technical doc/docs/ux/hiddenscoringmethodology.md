# Scoring and Feedback

The simulation records each decision across [five strategic dimensions](lxd/scoredimensions.md). Scores remain hidden during decision-making and are presented as reflective feedback at the designated strategic-profile screen.

## Score Updates

Each selected choice adds its configured effects to the cumulative scores:

**New cumulative score = previous cumulative score + choice-specific score effect**

Choice effects range from 0 to 4. 

## Participant-Facing Feedback

During normal decision-making, participants do not see:

- Running score totals
- Choice-specific point effects
- Archetype classification thresholds

At the strategic-profile screen, participants see:

- Five labeled strategic dimensions
- A percentage continuum for each dimension
- Earned points and maximum available points across completed nodes
- An explanation that results describe decision patterns, not grades

The percentage is calculated as:

**Earned points ÷ maximum available points across completed nodes × 100**

These percentages are not percentile rankings and do not compare participants with other teams.

## Narrative Feedback

Curveballs communicate changes in the business environment. Midpoint archetype diagnostics and final debriefs interpret the team’s strategic approach and highlight tradeoffs or implementation priorities.

A lower dimension score may reflect a deliberate emphasis on other priorities. No single profile is presented as the universally correct strategy.
