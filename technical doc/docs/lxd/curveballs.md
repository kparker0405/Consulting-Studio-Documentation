# Curveballs

Curveballs introduce changing business conditions that require participants to reassess assumptions, evaluate tradeoffs, and adapt their recommendations.

The events are predefined, and are designed to support decision-making under uncertainty (VUCA).

## Event Sequence

| Event | Timing | Instructional purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Passenger rail announcement | Beginning of Level 2 | Introduce a shared tourism opportunity and prompt teams to reconsider positioning, partnerships, and investment |
| Assigned business development | Beginning of Level 3 | Stress-test the emerging recommendation against a new opportunity, constraint, or disruption |

The second event is selected from 15 possible curveballs organized into four categories:

- Tourist Access & Market Competition
- Brand Equity & Community Dynamics
- Cost Pressures & Operating Guardrails
- Corporate Sponsorship & Revenue Shifts

## Learning Objectives

The events encourage participants to:

- Distinguish facts from assumptions
- Evaluate whether new information warrants a strategic change
- Balance growth, feasibility, and stakeholder interests
- Explain the reasoning behind their response
- Maintain team alignment under pressure

## Assignment and Debrief

Group assignments are determined by Team ID, providing a consistent event for each team. Sandbox assignments are randomized for each new playthrough and retained within that response.

Different events create opportunities for comparing how teams interpret uncertainty and justify their decisions.
