# Simulation Structure

The simulation follows a consulting engagement across four levels, completed on Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Tuesday. Each level represents a new phase of the project.

Each level contains **five sequential decision nodes**, with **five response options per node**. Between levels, the storyline advances by several weeks of simulated time, allowing research, stakeholder conversations, and business developments to unfold realistically.

Participants receive the same foundational [business case](lxd/businesscase.md). Their decisions shape their strategic direction, contribute to hidden performance scores, and influence the final simulation outcome.

The simulation follows a **shared decision sequence**, rather than a fully branching decision tree. Participants generally encounter the same decision questions, but assigned curveballs and accumulated choices create different challenges and strategic paths.

| Level | Scheduled day | Engagement phase | Primary learning emphasis |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wednesday | Defining the engagement and establishing team practices | Problem framing, research planning, stakeholder alignment, and collaboration |
| 2 | Thursday | Developing and evaluating a strategic direction | Responding to new information, defining value, financial analysis, and sponsor communication |
| 3 | Friday | Adapting under pressure | Managing disruption, balancing stakeholder interests, and coordinating work |
| 4 | Tuesday | Final recommendation and implementation planning | Strategic synthesis, financial justification, executive communication, and actionable delivery |

## Curveball Events

Participants encounter two assigned [curveball](ux/curveballmethodology.md) events during the engagement. 

Curveballs introduce information that may reinforce or challenge the team's assumptions. They do not automatically require a strategic pivot. Participants must decide whether to preserve their direction, revise their assumptions, rebalance priorities, or change implementation.

## Decision Logs and Recaps

In group mode, [daily decision logs](ux/decisionlog.md) ask:

- What assumptions underlie your decision-making strategy?
- What information gaps do you detect right now about the case/organization?
- How did your team work to align on a strategy?

At the beginning of the next level, a recap presents selected information from the previous decision log and the team's recorded strategic direction.

These activities connect the four levels and make changes in reasoning visible over time. They do not reveal hidden scores.

## Group and Solo Progression

### Group mode

Teams discuss each decision and submit one shared response through a designated scribe.

After completing a level and its decision log, the team reaches a release checkpoint. The next level opens at **3:00 a.m. Eastern Time on the next scheduled simulation day**, according to the release schedule.

Teams return using their original team response link. Only the designated scribe should operate that response at a given time.

### Solo mode

Individual practice follows the same decision structure but bypasses team decision logs and daily stop points.

Solo practice is a separate response and does not establish or replace the team's official decisions.
